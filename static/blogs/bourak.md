# Why the bourak is supreme?

The main purpose of this post is to explain to you my dear friend, why the bourak is the greatest food ever

The bourak is probably available everywhere in the world (IDK I'll search later), however only a few persons know the actual value of this amazing food..

## What even is a bourak?

For the uninitiated: it's a fried pastry made from thin phyllo dough wrapped around a filling and folded into triangles. You bite into it and the entire thing shatters in the most satisfying way possible while hot oily filling leaks out and burns your tongue. It's worth every degree of that burn.

## The tiers of bourak

Not all bouraks are created equal. Here's the hierarchy:

**SS tier — Chicken/Egg**: You may think this is foolish but once you try it, you'll never bother to eat minced beef again.

**S tier — Meat (mhamsa/minced beef)**: The OG. Savory, heavy, fills you up like a hug from your grandmother. Very good with Shourba (at least mom says so).

**A tier — Cheese**: The crowd-pleaser. stretches across the room when you pull it apart. Perfect entry-level bourak for the uninitiated (my favorite below SS).

**B tier — Spinach & Cheese**: For the people who want to feel healthy while eating deep-fried dough. Valid, but you're lying to yourself.

**C tier — Potato**: It's fine. It's just.. fine. Nobody's excited about potato bourak, nobody's mad about it either. It exists.

**D tier — Egg & Tuna**: I'm sorry but who invited you. This is a crime against humanity and should be studied by the ICC.
